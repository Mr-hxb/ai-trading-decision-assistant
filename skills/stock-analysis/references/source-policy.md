# Source Policy

## Source Priority

Use the strongest available source for each claim:

1. Exchange filings, official company announcements, investor relations pages, and regulator disclosures.
2. Market data pages that show quote time, exchange, currency, and delayed/realtime status.
3. Reputable financial news and mainstream business media.
4. Broker or chart pages only when their source, timestamp, and market context are clear.

Avoid anonymous summaries, unsourced social posts, and copied snippets without an original source.

## Freshness

For current market analysis, record:

- Analysis date and time.
- Quote or market-data timestamp when available.
- Publication date for each news, filing, or announcement.
- Whether the quote is delayed, after-hours, pre-market, or from a closed session.

If freshness cannot be verified, say so and lower confidence.

## Citation Requirements

Every final answer must include source URLs for:

- Current quote or price context.
- Material company events.
- Filings or official announcements.
- News used in the analysis.
- Market or sector context used in the analysis.

Do not cite a source for a claim it does not support.

## Conflict Handling

If sources disagree:

- State the conflict plainly.
- Prefer official sources for company-specific facts.
- Prefer timestamped market data for quote facts.
- Lower confidence when the conflict affects the conclusion.

Do not smooth over conflicts or pick the more convenient source silently.
